This 3D print is based on character sketches by my brother. I modeled it, then sent the file to my university library for printing. I lightly sanded some parts of the model, glued parts that came unattached, applied acrylic paint, and sprayed a matte finish over the surface. 

Software/tools: Blender.

Collaborators: Will Harvey (initial sketch and design), UNA Collier Library (3D printing service for students).
