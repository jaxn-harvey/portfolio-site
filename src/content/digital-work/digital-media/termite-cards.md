As part of a Digital Media class assignment, I designed, printed, and cut out these custom face cards. As an added challenge, cards were required to demonstrate rotational symmetry. I used termites as my characters because 1) Termite colonies have many interesting roles such as kings, queens, soldiers, etc., and 2) Because termites are generally disliked, making them an unlikely but fun source of inspiration.

Software/tools: Adobe Illustrator.
